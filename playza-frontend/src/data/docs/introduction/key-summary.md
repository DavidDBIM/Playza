
# Key Summary

Playza is a **skill-based competitive gaming platform** where every outcome is determined by player performance — not luck or spending. Players compete across four contest formats (**H2H, SoloEarn, Tournaments, and Games**) using **$ZA**, our in-platform currency, and earn **PZA Points** for loyalty on top.

## What makes Playza different

Playza is built on a small set of clear principles:

| Principle | What it means for players |
| --- | --- |
| **Free to Play** | Anyone can join and play without putting money in. |
| **Skill to Earn** | Rewards are tied to performance, not pay-to-win mechanics. |
| **Transparency** | Contest rules, scoring, and prize pools are visible up-front. |
| **Local-First Payments** | Top up with the local payment methods players already use — Paystack, Opay, or bank transfer. |
| **Loyalty That Pays Back** | PZA Points reward you just for showing up and being active. |
| **Sustainable Economy** | $ZA is designed to keep the platform fair and self-sustaining, with a fair prize-pool algorithm from 5 to 50,000 players. |

## In one paragraph

Playza is a unified gaming arena that offers a polished, competitive experience across Head-to-Head duels, SoloEarn challenges, Tournaments, and time-based Games leaderboards. Players deposit using familiar local payment methods to receive **$ZA**, then enter contests across our catalog of 7+ live games. Winners are paid in $ZA and can withdraw back to local currency at a fixed rate. On top of that, every player earns **PZA Points** — a loyalty score that unlocks tiers, merchandise, and Ambassador status.

## Prize pools that scale fairly

The Playza prize pool algorithm scales fairly from **5 to 50,000 players**, ensuring that even bottom-tier winners earn meaningfully — not just the top few.

## Who Playza is for

- **Casual players** who want fun, quick games with the chance to win.
- **Competitive players** who want H2H matches, tournaments with big pools, and leaderboards.
- **Community members** who want to earn PZA Points, climb tiers, and become Ambassadors.
- **Game developers** who want a ready-made audience and monetization rails. See [Build with Playza](/doc/build-with-playza/developer-program).
