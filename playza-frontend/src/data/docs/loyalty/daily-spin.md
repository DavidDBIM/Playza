
# Daily Spin

Every player receives up to **3 spins per day** on the Playza reward wheel. Spins are a fast, fun way to top up your PZA Points balance daily.

Spin from your [loyalty dashboard](https://www.playza.games/loyalty).

## How it works

| Detail | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Spins available per day** | 3 spins |
| **Cost per spin** | 30 PZA Points |
| **Maximum win per spin** | 1,000 PZA Points |
| **Unused spins** | Do not carry over to the next day |

## The economics

- Each spin costs **30 PZA Points**.
- You can win up to **1,000 PZA Points** per spin.
- With 3 spins/day at 30 points each, that's **90 PZA Points spent** for a shot at up to **3,000 PZA Points**.

## Tips

- **Use your spins every day** — unused spins do not carry over.
- Combine spins with your **[daily streak bonus](/doc/loyalty/earning-points#2-daily-streak)** to maximise your daily PZA Points haul.
- Spin winnings count toward your **[tier progression](/doc/loyalty/tiers)**.

## FAQ

**Can I buy more spins?**  
No — every player gets the same 3 spins per day.

**Can I spin without any PZA Points?**  
You need at least 30 PZA Points to cover the cost of a spin. Complete a few [profile tasks](/doc/loyalty/earning-points#1-getting-started) or daily streaks to get started.

**What happens if I miss a day?**  
You lose that day's spins (they don't accumulate), and your daily streak resets to zero if you also miss the daily login. Show up daily to maximise both.
