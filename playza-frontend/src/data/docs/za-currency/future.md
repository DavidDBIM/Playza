
# The Future of $ZA

Today, $ZA is a **simple, fiat-funded in-platform currency**. That's a deliberate choice — it keeps the experience clean and accessible for every player. But the Web3 chapter of $ZA is on the roadmap, and it's **staged intentionally**.

Our Web3 roadmap is built **on top of a proven, working gaming economy** — not the other way around. We believe digital ownership should **enhance** gameplay, not replace it.

## The Web3 roadmap for $ZA

### 🏗️ Foundation — Q3 2026

| Milestone | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **$ZA Token Architecture** | Web3 foundation designed and finalized |
| **Web3 Wallet Integration** | Connect external digital wallets to your Playza account |

### 🚀 Launch — Q4 2026

| Milestone | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **$ZA Token Public Launch** | $ZA moves from in-platform currency to a fully live digital token |
| **First NFT Collection** | In-game items, achievement badges, and player collectibles as NFTs |
| **NFT Utility In-Game** | Owned NFTs usable across contests and game formats on the platform |
| **$ZA Exchange Listing** | $ZA token listed on a digital asset exchange for open trading |

See the full [Roadmap](/doc/playza-gaming/roadmap) for context.

## What this unlocks for players

- **True ownership** of in-game items and achievement badges via NFTs.
- **Portable value** — $ZA becomes tradeable on an exchange, not just spendable inside Playza.
- **Interoperability** with external wallets you already use.
- **New in-game utility** for NFTs across the four contest formats.

## What isn't changing

- Playza is, and will remain, a **skill-based competitive gaming platform**. Web3 is additive.
- Contest rules, scoring, and prize pool fairness stay the same.
- Local payment options (Paystack, Opay, bank transfer) continue to work for players who prefer fiat.
- Playing without touching Web3 will always be an option.

## How we'll decide

Every step of the Web3 roll-out is evaluated against three questions:

1. **Does it make the player experience better?**
2. **Does it preserve fairness and skill-based play?**
3. **Does it meet the legal and regulatory expectations in our markets?**

If the answer to all three is yes, we ship it. If not, we hold.
