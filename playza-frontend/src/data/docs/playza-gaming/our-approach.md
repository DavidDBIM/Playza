
# Our Approach

This page explains **how we think about building Playza** — the design principles that guide every product decision, game pick, and feature.

## 1. Player-first, always

We build for the player who's there to have fun. That means:

- No dark patterns. No fake "limited time!" timers.
- No pay-to-win advantages in any contest.
- Honest odds, dynamic payout curves, and clear prize structures.
- Withdrawals are as easy as deposits — up to 9,999 $ZA is instant.

## 2. Skill over luck

Playza is a **skill-based** platform. Every contest is designed so that better play leads to better outcomes. Where a game has a small random element (e.g., piece order in a puzzle), the same randomness applies to **every player in the contest** — so results still come down to skill.

## 3. Fair prize pools that scale

The Playza prize pool algorithm scales fairly from **5 to 50,000 players**. Big or small, the payout curve ensures even bottom-tier winners earn meaningfully — you don't have to be top-1% to walk away with $ZA.

## 4. Local-first payments

We meet players where they are. Instead of forcing a single global payment rail, Playza supports the **local payment systems** players already use day-to-day:

- Paystack
- Opay
- Bank transfer

More international payment support is on the [roadmap](/doc/playza-gaming/roadmap) for Q2 2026.

## 5. Loyalty that pays back

Beyond winning $ZA, players earn **PZA Points** just for showing up — daily streaks, referrals, social tasks, and rank-ups all feed into a loyalty score that unlocks merch and Ambassador perks. See [PZA Points & Loyalty](/doc/loyalty/overview).

## 6. Transparency by default

- Every contest's rules, entry, and prize structure are visible **before** you enter.
- Every wallet transaction is logged in your history.
- Every scoring system is published.
- Deposit rate is fixed and shown before you confirm: **1,000 $ZA = 1,000 Naira = ~$0.73 USD**.

## 7. Mobile-first, but not mobile-only

The Playza web experience at [playza.games](https://www.playza.games/) works across devices, and mobile optimisation is a **Q2 2026** roadmap item. A dedicated mobile app is on the way.

## 8. Build the platform, partner on games

Playza focuses on the platform layer: accounts, contests, wallet, payments, leaderboards, integrity, loyalty. We partner with talented developers and studios for great games.

If you're a developer or a studio, see **[Build with Playza](/doc/build-with-playza/developer-program)**.

## 9. Web3, staged intentionally

Our Web3 roadmap is intentionally staged: the **$ZA token, NFT collection, and exchange listing are targeted for Q4 2026**, built on top of a **proven, working gaming economy** — not the other way around. We believe digital ownership should enhance gameplay, not replace it. See [The Future of $ZA](/doc/za-currency/future).
