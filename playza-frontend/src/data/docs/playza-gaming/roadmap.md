## How we plan

- Roadmaps are directional — we ship what's working and revise what isn't.
- Player feedback shapes every priority decision.
- Every item in the **Shipped** column is verifiable in the live product at [playza.games](https://www.playza.games/).
- Our Web3 roadmap is intentionally staged — the **$ZA token and NFT layer are built on top of a proven, working gaming economy**, not the other way around. We believe digital ownership should enhance gameplay, not replace it.
