
# Core Gameplay

Playza is a **skill-based competitive gaming platform** where every outcome is determined by player performance — not luck or spending.

Every contest belongs to one of **four formats**. They all use the same $ZA wallet, the same account, and the same anti-cheat system underneath.

## The four contest formats

### ⚔️ H2H — Head to Head

Two players. One winner.

Players pay an entry fee in **$ZA**, compete on the same game, and the **higher score takes the prize pool minus the platform fee**.

- Best for: Direct one-on-one competition.
- Play now → [playza.games/h2h](https://www.playza.games/h2h)

### 🎯 SoloEarn

A solo challenge where a player competes against a **set target score**. Beat the target, earn $ZA. Simple, fast, and accessible for any player at any time.

- Best for: Quick sessions on your own schedule.
- Play now → [playza.games/solo-earn](https://www.playza.games/solo-earn)

### 🏆 Tournaments

Multi-player contests where everyone pays an entry fee into a **shared prize pool**. The top finishers win based on a **dynamic payout curve** — the bigger the tournament, the more winners.

The Playza prize pool algorithm scales fairly from **5 to 50,000 players**, ensuring even bottom-tier winners earn meaningfully.

- Best for: Bigger prize pools, more competition.
- Play now → [playza.games/tournaments](https://www.playza.games/tournaments)

### 🕹️ Games (Leaderboards)

Players compete over a set time period. Rankings are based on **cumulative score**. Top players at the end of the period win $ZA rewards.

- Best for: Sustained competitive play over time.
- Play now → [playza.games/games](https://www.playza.games/games)

## The basic loop

1. **Pick a format** — H2H, SoloEarn, Tournament, or Games.
2. **Pick a game** from the Playza catalog.
3. **Enter** using $ZA from your wallet (some formats have a free / practice mode).
4. **Play** the round. Your score is recorded server-side.
5. **Get ranked** against everyone else who played that contest.
6. **Earn $ZA** based on where you finish — and PZA Points on top for loyalty.

## Scoring & fairness

- Every game has a **transparent scoring system**, published on its [Rules & Scoring](/doc/help/rules-and-scoring) page.
- Scores are recorded server-side and cannot be tampered with by the client.
- Within a single contest, every player faces the **same challenge**.

## What "skill-based" actually means

A contest is skill-based when **better play leads to better outcomes**. Across the Playza catalog, this means:

- The same game state is presented to every player in a contest.
- Outcome is determined by player decisions, reflexes, or strategy.
- There is no "buy a better outcome" mechanic during the match.

> If a contest can't be made skill-based, it doesn't ship on Playza.
