
# Welcome to Playza

**Playza is a skill-based competitive gaming platform where players compete in casual games and win real rewards.** Whether you're here for a quick match, a tournament, or to build something new with us, this is your starting point.

This documentation covers everything you need to know about Playza — what it is, how it works, the **$ZA** in-platform currency, the **PZA Points** loyalty program, our games, and how developers can build on the platform.

## What you'll find here

- **[Introduction](/doc/introduction/key-summary)** — A high-level summary of Playza, the problem we're solving, and where the platform fits in the market.
- **[Playza Gaming](/doc/playza-gaming/core-gameplay)** — How gameplay works, contest formats (H2H, SoloEarn, Tournaments, Games), our catalog, and the product roadmap.
- **[$ZA Currency](/doc/za-currency/what-is-za)** — Everything about $ZA: how to top up, how it converts, how withdrawals work, and what's next.
- **[PZA Points & Loyalty](/doc/loyalty/overview)** — Earn PZA Points, climb Bronze → Platinum tiers, spin daily, redeem merch, and become a Playza Ambassador.
- **[Build with Playza](/doc/build-with-playza/developer-program)** — For developers and studios who want to publish games on Playza.
- **[Help & FAQs](/doc/help/knowledge-base)** — Rules, scoring, common questions, responsible gaming, and legal info.
