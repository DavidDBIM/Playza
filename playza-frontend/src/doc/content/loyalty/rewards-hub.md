## How to redeem

1. Open your [loyalty dashboard](https://www.playza.games/loyalty).
2. Browse the available rewards.
3. Tap the item you want and confirm the redemption.
4. Enter your shipping details.
5. Your item ships from Playza directly to you.

## Can I convert PZA Points to $ZA?

No — PZA Points are a loyalty reward, not a currency substitute. They can only be redeemed for merchandise and platform rewards.

If you want to earn cash-out-able rewards, play contests and win **$ZA** — see [Core Gameplay](/doc/playza-gaming/core-gameplay).
