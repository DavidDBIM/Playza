
# PZA Points & Loyalty Program

**PZA Points are Playza's loyalty reward system.**

While **$ZA** is the currency you use to play and win, **PZA Points** are what you earn simply by being an active member of the Playza community — logging in daily, completing tasks, referring friends, and engaging with the platform.

Your loyalty dashboard lives at [playza.games/loyalty](https://www.playza.games/loyalty).

## PZA Points ≠ $ZA

**PZA Points and $ZA are completely separate.**

- **$ZA** is your gaming wallet used to enter contests and collect winnings. See [$ZA Currency](/doc/za-currency/what-is-za).
- **PZA Points** are your loyalty score — earned through activity, redeemable for rewards.

| | **$ZA** | **PZA Points** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | In-platform currency | Loyalty score |
| How you get it | Deposit or win contests | Earned through activity |
| Can you withdraw as cash? | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
| Can you buy it directly? | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
| What you spend it on | Contest entries | Playza merchandise |

## How the program works

1. **Earn points** through every action on Playza — signing up, daily streaks, playing, referring, socials, and more.
2. **Climb tiers** as your points accumulate: Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum.
3. **Redeem rewards** — Playza merchandise, straight from your loyalty dashboard.
4. **Level up further** — Gold-tier and above can apply to become official **Playza Ambassadors**.

## What PZA Points ARE — and AREN'T

| ✅ PZA Points **ARE** | ❌ PZA Points **ARE NOT** |
| --- | --- |
| A loyalty reward for platform activity | A substitute for $ZA gaming currency |
| Redeemable for merchandise and platform rewards | Withdrawable as cash or real money |
| Earned through gameplay, tasks, and referrals | Purchasable directly with money |
| A measure of your standing in the community | A speculative or tradeable asset |
