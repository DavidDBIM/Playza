
# Ambassador Program

Top players and community leaders with **Gold PZA tier and above** can apply to become an official **Playza Ambassador**.

Ambassadors are the face of the Playza community — earning exclusive perks, additional PZA Points, and the opportunity to represent the platform publicly.

## Who qualifies

- 🥇 **Gold-tier** PZA loyalty status or above (25,000+ PZA Points).
- Active, positive community presence.
- Aligned with Playza's mission: fair play, skill-based competition, and community-first growth.

Check your tier on your [loyalty dashboard](https://www.playza.games/loyalty).

## Ambassador benefits

- 🏅 **Official Ambassador badge** displayed on your player profile
- 💎 **Additional PZA Points** earnings on all platform activity
- 🚀 **Early access** to new games, features, and platform updates
- 🎟️ **Invitations** to exclusive Playza events and tournaments
- 🤝 **Co-marketing opportunities** with the Playza team

## How to apply

Once you hit **Gold tier** or above:

1. Head to your [loyalty dashboard](https://www.playza.games/loyalty).
2. Look for the **Ambassador Program** application section.
3. Submit your application — tell us why you'd be a great Ambassador and how you engage with the community.

You can also reach out directly at [Admin@playza.games](mailto:Admin@playza.games) with the subject line **"Ambassador Application"**.

## What we look for

- **Community presence** — you're already engaging with other players on social, in the app, or IRL.
- **Positive tone** — you build the community up, not tear it down.
- **Alignment** — you get what Playza is about (skill, fairness, community over hype).
- **Reach or influence** doesn't hurt — but it isn't required.

## What Ambassadors do

- Represent Playza in your community and on social.
- Help onboard new players.
- Host casual community tournaments or watch-alongs.
- Share feedback and product ideas directly with the Playza team.
- Rep the badge on your profile.

> 🌟 The Ambassador Program is our way of thanking the people making Playza a better place to play.
