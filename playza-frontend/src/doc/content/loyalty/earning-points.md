
# How to Earn PZA Points

There are multiple ways to earn PZA Points on Playza — **many of them completely free**. The more active you are on the platform, the faster you accumulate points and climb tiers.

## 1. Getting Started

Complete your profile setup to earn your first batch of PZA Points. These are one-time tasks available to every new player.

| Task | Points Earned |
| --- | --- |
| Join the Playza ecosystem | +5 PZA |
| Verify your email address | +10 PZA |
| Verify your phone number | +10 PZA |
| Upload your profile picture | +20 PZA |
| Complete your profile bio and details | +30 PZA |
| **Total available** | **+75 PZA** |

## 2. Daily Streak

Log in every day and claim your streak bonus. The longer your streak, the bigger the reward — with a major bonus on **Day 7**.

| Day | Streak Bonus |
| --- | --- |
| Day 2 | +10 PZA |
| Day 3 | +30 PZA |
| Day 4 | +10 PZA |
| Day 5 | +10 PZA |
| Day 6 | +10 PZA |
| **Day 7 ⭐** | **+80 PZA** |
| Day 8+ | +10 PZA |

> 📌 Missing a single day resets your streak back to zero. Claim your daily bonus every day to protect your streak and maximise rewards.

## 3. Play & Win

Earn PZA Points by actively competing across all contest formats on the platform. Simply playing and winning contributes to your loyalty score.

| Format | How You Earn |
| --- | --- |
| **H2H — Head to Head** | Earn PZA Points for every match played and won |
| **SoloEarn** | Earn PZA Points for beating target scores |
| **Tournaments** | Earn bonus PZA Points for participating and placing |
| **Games** | Earn PZA Points for finishing in ranked positions |

> Winning contests also pays out **$ZA** — see [Core Gameplay](/doc/playza-gaming/core-gameplay) for the $ZA prize side of things.

## 4. Refer Friends

Earn PZA Points for every friend you successfully refer to Playza. **Up to 15 referral milestones** are available — the more players you bring in, the more points you earn.

## 5. Social Tasks

Complete community and social media tasks to earn PZA Points and help grow the Playza ecosystem. Tasks are updated regularly on your [loyalty dashboard](https://www.playza.games/loyalty).

## 6. Rank Up

Hit loyalty milestones and tier thresholds to earn bonus PZA Points as you progress through the program. Every tier upgrade comes with a points reward. See [Loyalty Tiers](/doc/loyalty/tiers).

## 7. Daily Spin

Up to 3 spins per day on the Playza reward wheel — with a max of 1,000 PZA per spin. See **[Daily Spin](/doc/loyalty/daily-spin)** for the full rules.

## Ready to redeem?

Once you've stacked up PZA Points, head to the **[Rewards Hub](/doc/loyalty/rewards-hub)** to swap them for Playza merchandise.
