
# Loyalty Tiers

As you accumulate PZA Points, you climb through loyalty tiers. Higher tiers unlock better rewards, priority access, and exclusive platform perks.

Your current tier and progress are always visible on your loyalty dashboard at [playza.games/loyalty](https://www.playza.games/loyalty).

## The tiers

| Tier | Points Required | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🥉 **Bronze** | Starting tier — all new players begin here | Entry |
| 🥈 **Silver** | Up to 5,000 PZA | Progressing |
| 🥇 **Gold** | Up to 25,000 PZA | Advanced |
| 💎 **Platinum** | 100,000 PZA and above | Elite |

## What each tier unlocks

Higher tiers unlock:

- Better and more rewards in the [Rewards Hub](/doc/loyalty/rewards-hub)
- Priority access to new features and games
- Exclusive platform perks
- **Gold-tier and above** can apply to become an official **[Playza Ambassador](/doc/loyalty/ambassador)**

## How to climb

Every PZA Point you earn counts toward your tier. The fastest ways to climb:

- **Daily streaks** — up to +80 PZA on Day 7 (and it resets if you miss a day).
- **Refer friends** — up to 15 referral milestones available.
- **Play & win** contests across H2H, SoloEarn, Tournaments, and Games.
- **Social tasks** — updated regularly on your loyalty dashboard.
- **Rank ups** — hit milestones and earn bonus PZA Points on tier upgrades.

See the full list in **[How to Earn PZA Points](/doc/loyalty/earning-points)**.
