## What every Playza game has in common

- **Quick rounds** — most matches finish in a couple of minutes.
- **Fair start** — every player in a contest gets the same challenge.
- **Clear scoring** — score is on-screen, in real time.
- **Same wallet** — your $ZA balance works in every game.
- **PZA Points on top** — every match earns loyalty points, regardless of the outcome.
