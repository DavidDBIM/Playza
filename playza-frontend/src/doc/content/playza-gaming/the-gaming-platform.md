
# The Gaming Platform

Underneath the games, Playza is a **gaming platform** — the shared infrastructure that lets any title plug in and offer fair, skill-based contests with real rewards.

## What the platform provides

| Layer | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Player accounts** | A single Playza identity that works across every game in the catalog. |
| **$ZA wallet** | One balance, used to enter contests, receive winnings, and withdraw. |
| **Contest engine** | Creates and runs H2H, SoloEarn, Tournament, and Games (leaderboard) contests across every title. |
| **Prize pool algorithm** | Scales fairly from 5 to 50,000 players so even bottom-tier winners earn meaningfully. |
| **Matchmaking** | Matches players into the right contest based on skill and stakes. |
| **Leaderboards** | Real-time, transparent rankings for every active contest. |
| **Payments** | Deposits and withdrawals through Paystack, Opay, and bank transfer. |
| **Anti-cheat & integrity** | Server-side scoring, anomaly detection, and review tooling. |
| **PZA Points** | Loyalty engine that runs alongside every action — see [Loyalty](/doc/loyalty/overview). |

## A unified player experience

Players don't switch apps, accounts, or wallets to move between games. Everything — your $ZA balance, your PZA Points, your friends, your rewards history, your stats — lives in one Playza account.

## A single platform fee

Playza takes a small, transparent platform fee on each paid contest. The rest of the prize pool is paid out to winners.

> The fee is shown to players before they enter a contest. No hidden cuts.

## Cross-platform from day one

- 💻 **Web** — the primary Playza experience today: [playza.games](https://www.playza.games/)
- 📱 **Mobile app** — *coming soon*

## Built for developers

Game developers don't need to build accounts, wallets, payments, or contests — Playza provides all of that. Developers focus on the **game**; Playza handles the platform.

See **[Build with Playza](/doc/build-with-playza/developer-program)** for the developer experience.
