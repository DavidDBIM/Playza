
# Playza App

The Playza web app at [playza.games](https://www.playza.games/) is the main way to play today. A **dedicated mobile app is coming soon**, and mobile optimisation for the web experience is a Q2 2026 roadmap item.

## What you can do on Playza today

- 🎮 Browse and play every game in the Playza catalog
- ⚔️ Enter H2H, SoloEarn, Tournament, and Games (leaderboard) contests
- 💰 Top up your $ZA balance via **Paystack, Opay, or bank transfer**
- 💸 Withdraw winnings back to your local payment method (instant up to 9,999 $ZA)
- 🎁 Earn **PZA Points** and climb Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum tiers
- 🎰 Spin the daily reward wheel (up to 3 spins/day)
- 🛍️ Redeem PZA Points for Playza merchandise
- 📈 Track your stats, rank, and full transaction history
- 👯 Refer friends and earn $ZA + PZA Points
