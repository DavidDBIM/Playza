## Highlights

Playza is a skill-based competitive gaming platform where players compete across **H2H, SoloEarn, Tournaments, and Leaderboards** using **$ZA**, our in-platform currency. The prize pool algorithm scales fairly from **5 to 50,000 players**, ensuring even bottom-tier winners earn meaningfully.

The platform is expanding globally with more games, studio collaborations, and international payment support. On the Web3 side, the **$ZA token and an NFT collection are targeted for Q4 2026**, with an exchange listing to follow.

Growth is driven by a **referral program**, community building, influencer partnerships, and sponsored tournaments — all designed to acquire and retain players at scale.
