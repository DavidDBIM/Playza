
# Market

## A massive, growing opportunity

Casual and skill-based mobile gaming is one of the largest entertainment categories in the world. Playza sits at the intersection of four powerful trends:

1. **Mobile-first gaming** — most new players today start (and stay) on mobile.
2. **Skill-based competitive contests** — players want their effort to translate into real outcomes.
3. **Local digital payments** — the rapid rise of regional payment systems (Paystack, Opay, bank transfers) makes "deposit & withdraw" feel as easy as sending a message.
4. **Digital assets & Web3** — $ZA is built with a future-ready vision: integration with digital currencies and Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs), expanding how players own, vote, trade, and earn within the Playza ecosystem.

## The audience we're built for

| Segment | What they want | How Playza serves them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Casual mobile gamers | Quick, fun sessions | 7+ live games across Arcade, Puzzle, and Strategy |
| Skilled competitive players | Leaderboards, tournaments, real prizes | H2H, Tournaments with dynamic payout curves, and time-based Games |
| Underbanked / cash-first regions | Easy local payment rails | Paystack, Opay, and bank transfers |
| Community & loyalty seekers | Recognition, tiers, perks | PZA Points, Bronze → Platinum tiers, Ambassador program |
| Game developers & studios | Distribution + monetization | Publish on Playza, share in contest revenue |

## Why now

- Mobile gaming has matured: production quality is high, but player satisfaction is at an all-time low because of monetization fatigue.
- Local digital payment networks are now mainstream in most of the regions we target, making real-money deposits and withdrawals frictionless.
- A new generation of players expects transparency, fairness, and a fair shot — not lootboxes and dark patterns.
- Web3 and digital ownership are moving from speculation to real utility, and Playza's roadmap positions $ZA and NFTs (Q4 2026) on top of a **proven, working gaming economy** — not the other way around.

## Our positioning

Playza is **not** a casino, **not** a sportsbook, and **not** a pay-to-win game. It is a **skill-based competitive gaming platform** with a transparent in-platform currency ($ZA), a loyalty program (PZA Points), and clear rules around every contest.

> We compete with boredom — and with the dark patterns of mainstream free-to-play.
