
# Withdrawals

You can convert your $ZA back to **local currency** at any time, through the same payment rails you used to deposit.

Manage withdrawals from [playza.games/wallet](https://www.playza.games/wallet).

## How to withdraw

1. Open your [wallet](https://www.playza.games/wallet) and tap **Withdraw**.
2. Choose your destination (bank account, Paystack, or Opay).
3. Enter the amount in $ZA — the app shows the local-currency equivalent before you confirm.
4. Confirm. The payout is processed and lands in your destination.

## The conversion rate

Withdrawals use the same fixed rate as deposits:

> **1,000 $ZA = ₦1,000 (Naira) ≈ $0.73 USD**

Sample amounts:

| You withdraw | You receive |
| --- | --- |
| 1,000 $ZA | ₦1,000 |
| 5,000 $ZA | ₦5,000 |
| 9,999 $ZA | ₦9,999 (instant payout) |
| 10,000 $ZA | ₦10,000 (admin approval required) |

## Limits, fees, and timing

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Minimum withdrawal** | 1,000 $ZA |
| **Instant payout threshold** | Up to 9,999 $ZA |
| **Above 10,000 $ZA** | Requires admin approval for security |
| **Max withdrawals per day** | 3 withdrawals |
| **Withdrawal fee** | None |

## Instant vs. admin-approved payouts

- **Instant (1,000 – 9,999 $ZA):** processed immediately once you confirm.
- **Admin-approved (≥ 10,000 $ZA):** held for a review by the Playza admin team as an anti-fraud measure. You'll see it as "pending review" in your wallet history and be notified once processed.

> This tiered flow keeps small, everyday withdrawals fast while adding an extra safety check on large ones.

## Anti-fraud & integrity

- Withdrawals are subject to anti-fraud checks.
- Repeated failed attempts, mismatched destination details, or suspicious activity can lead to temporary holds.
- If your account is in good standing and details match, withdrawals move quickly.
- See [Knowledge Base](/doc/help/knowledge-base) → "My withdrawal is stuck" for help.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Withdrawal hasn't arrived (instant) | Give it a few minutes, then check your wallet history. If still stuck, contact [Admin@playza.games](mailto:Admin@playza.games). |
| Withdrawal held for admin approval | Normal for amounts ≥ 10,000 $ZA. You'll be notified when it's processed. |
| Destination details wrong | Withdrawals can't be re-routed after the fact. Contact [Admin@playza.games](mailto:Admin@playza.games). |
| Hit the daily withdrawal cap | You get up to 3 withdrawals per day. Try again tomorrow, or bundle amounts. |
| Withdrawal rejected | Check the reason in wallet history; see [FAQs](/doc/help/faqs). |
