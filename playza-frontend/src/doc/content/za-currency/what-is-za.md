
# What is $ZA?

**$ZA is the in-platform currency of Playza.** It's how players enter paid contests, how winnings are paid out, and how value moves around inside the Playza ecosystem.

Your $ZA balance and full transaction history are always available at [playza.games/wallet](https://www.playza.games/wallet).

## The rate

> **1,000 $ZA = ₦1,000 (Nigerian Naira) ≈ $0.73 USD**

The rate is fixed and shown in the app every time you top up or withdraw.

## What $ZA is — and isn't (today)

| ✅ $ZA **is** | ❌ $ZA **is not (yet)** |
| --- | --- |
| An in-platform currency used to enter and win Playza contests | A cryptocurrency or token (Q4 2026 target for public token launch) |
| Backed 1:1 by real value held by Playza | An investment or speculative asset |
| Convertible to local currency via withdrawals | Traded on any external exchange |
| Stored in your Playza wallet | A loyalty / points system — that's [PZA Points](/doc/loyalty/overview) |

> 🎯 The **$ZA token public launch and exchange listing** are targeted for **Q4 2026**. See [The Future of $ZA](/doc/za-currency/future).

## Why use $ZA at all (vs. just charging cash per contest)?

$ZA solves three real problems:

1. **One balance, many games.** Top up once, play across every game in the catalog.
2. **Fewer payment fees.** You pay payment-rail fees on deposit/withdraw, not on every single contest entry.
3. **A clear in-app economy.** Contest entries, winnings, and refunds all use a single, consistent unit.

## How $ZA enters and leaves the platform

```
                  ┌────────────────────┐
   Local cash  →  │  Deposit / Top-up  │ →  $ZA in your Playza wallet
                  └────────────────────┘

   $ZA in wallet →  Enter a contest  →  Win  → $ZA in wallet
                                       Lose → $ZA goes to the prize pool

                  ┌────────────────────┐
   $ZA in wallet →│      Withdraw      │ →  Local cash in your account
                  └────────────────────┘
```

## $ZA vs. PZA Points

These are two different things — don't confuse them:

| | **$ZA** | **PZA Points** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | In-platform currency | Loyalty score |
| How you get it | Deposit or win contests | Earned through activity |
| Can you withdraw as cash? | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
| Can you buy it directly? | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
| What you spend it on | Contest entries | Playza merchandise |

See [PZA Points & Loyalty](/doc/loyalty/overview) for the full loyalty program.
