
# Deposits & Top-ups

Topping up $ZA on Playza is designed to be as **easy and local** as possible — no crypto, no international cards required.

Manage your wallet at [playza.games/wallet](https://www.playza.games/wallet).

## The basic flow

1. Open your [wallet](https://www.playza.games/wallet) and tap **Top Up**.
2. Choose your payment method (Paystack, Opay, or bank transfer).
3. Enter the amount you want to deposit.
4. Confirm — your $ZA balance updates as soon as the payment is confirmed.

## Supported payment methods

| Method | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Paystack** | Cards, bank, USSD via Paystack. |
| **Opay** | Fast local payments via Opay. |
| **Bank Transfer** | Direct transfer to Playza's collection account. |

> 🌍 More international payment methods are on the [roadmap](/doc/playza-gaming/roadmap) for **Q2 2026** as part of our global expansion.

## The conversion rate

**1,000 $ZA = ₦1,000 (Naira) ≈ $0.73 USD**

Sample amounts:

| You deposit | You receive |
| --- | --- |
| ₦1,000 | 1,000 $ZA |
| ₦5,000 | 5,000 $ZA |
| ₦10,000 | 10,000 $ZA |
| ₦100,000 | 100,000 $ZA |

## Limits & fees

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Minimum deposit** | 1,000 $ZA (₦1,000) |
| **Maximum deposit per day** | 10,000,000 $ZA |
| **Deposit fee** | 1% |

The deposit fee is applied on top of the amount you're depositing and is shown clearly before you confirm.

## What if my deposit doesn't show up?

- Most deposits arrive within minutes.
- If your deposit is still missing after a reasonable wait, open the failed transaction in your wallet history and reach out to [Admin@playza.games](mailto:Admin@playza.games).
- See **[Knowledge Base](/doc/help/knowledge-base)** for common deposit issues.

## Security

- Payments are processed through trusted providers (Paystack, Opay) and bank rails — Playza never sees your card or bank credentials directly.
- Your Playza account balance sits behind an authenticated login — enable strong login security on your account.
