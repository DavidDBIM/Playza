
# Developer Program

Build a game. Reach Playza's players. Share in the revenue.

The Playza Developer Program is how independent studios and developers get their games in front of our audience — without having to build accounts, wallets, payments, or contest infrastructure.

These docs are actively being expanded with deeper developer-specific content (**Q1 2026** milestone on our [roadmap](/doc/playza-gaming/roadmap)).

## Who we're looking for

- Studios with **casual, skill-based** game ideas across **Arcade, Puzzle, or Strategy** (like our current catalog).
- Solo developers shipping polished, focused games.
- Teams porting an existing title to one of our four contest formats.

## What "skill-based" means for developers

For a game to ship on Playza, it must be possible to run it as a **fair, skill-based contest** — where every player in a contest faces the same challenge and the better player wins more often than not.

## Process at a glance

1. **Apply** via [Submit Your Game](/doc/build-with-playza/submit-your-game).
2. **Initial review.** Our team checks the concept fit and reviews any playable build.
3. **Technical integration.** You integrate the Playza SDK / API (see [Technical Documentation](/doc/build-with-playza/technical-documentation)).
4. **Test contests.** We run private test contests with a small group of players.
5. **Soft launch.** Your game appears in the Playza catalog for a subset of players.
6. **Full launch.** Once the metrics are healthy, your game is rolled out to everyone.

## How revenue works

Playza takes a transparent platform fee from each paid contest. The rest is split between **the prize pool** (paid to winners) and **the developer share**.
