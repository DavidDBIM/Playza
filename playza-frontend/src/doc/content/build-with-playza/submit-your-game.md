
# Submit Your Game

Ready to put your game in front of Playza's players? Here's exactly what we'll need from you.

## Before you apply

Make sure your game fits Playza's catalog:

- ✅ It's **skill-based** — better play leads to better outcomes.
- ✅ Rounds are **short** (typically 1–5 minutes).
- ✅ It fits one of the **four contest formats**: H2H, SoloEarn, Tournaments, or Games (leaderboards).
- ✅ It's a fit for one of our existing genres — **Arcade, Puzzle, or Strategy** — or makes a strong case to expand.
- ✅ You can submit final scores **server-side** (no purely client-trusted scoring).

If you're not sure, apply anyway — we're happy to help shape the contest format with you.

## What to include in your submission

1. **Your team** — names, roles, links to past work.
2. **Game overview** — one-paragraph pitch.
3. **A playable build** — APK, web build, TestFlight, or anything we can actually try.
4. **Proposed contest format(s)** — which of H2H / SoloEarn / Tournament / Games best fits the game.
5. **Scoring system** — exactly how points are awarded.
6. **Region & platforms** — where you intend to launch and on which devices.
7. **Funding interest** — if you're also interested in [Grants & Investments](/doc/build-with-playza/grants-and-investments).

4. **Soft launch** → **full launch**.
