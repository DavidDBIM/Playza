## Wallet & $ZA

### Where is my $ZA balance?

Open your wallet at [playza.games/wallet](https://www.playza.games/wallet). Your $ZA balance is visible there, along with your full transaction history.

### What's the $ZA rate?

**1,000 $ZA = ₦1,000 = ~$0.73 USD.** Same rate for deposits and withdrawals.

### My deposit hasn't arrived

Most deposits (Paystack, Opay, bank transfer) arrive within minutes. If yours is still missing after a reasonable wait, contact [Admin@playza.games](mailto:Admin@playza.games) with the transaction reference.

### My withdrawal is stuck

- **Instant payouts** (up to 9,999 $ZA) are usually near-immediate. If it hasn't landed in a few minutes, contact support.
- **Withdrawals of 10,000 $ZA or more** require **admin approval** for security. This can take a bit longer — you'll be notified in-app when it's processed.
- You can only make **3 withdrawals per day**.

See [Withdrawals → Troubleshooting](/doc/za-currency/withdrawals#troubleshooting).

### Are there any fees?

- **Deposits:** 1% deposit fee.
- **Withdrawals:** No withdrawal fee.
- **Contest entries:** A small, transparent platform fee per contest, shown before you confirm.

## PZA Points & Loyalty

### Are PZA Points the same as $ZA?

No. See **[PZA Points & Loyalty — Overview](/doc/loyalty/overview)** for the full breakdown.

### Can I withdraw PZA Points as cash?

No. PZA Points are redeemable for [Playza merchandise](/doc/loyalty/rewards-hub), not cash.

### I missed a day — did I lose my streak?

Yes. Missing a single day resets your daily streak to zero. See [How to Earn PZA Points → Daily Streak](/doc/loyalty/earning-points#2-daily-streak).

### How do I check my tier?

Your tier and points progress are shown at [playza.games/loyalty](https://www.playza.games/loyalty). See [Loyalty Tiers](/doc/loyalty/tiers).

## Contests & gameplay

### What's the difference between H2H, SoloEarn, Tournaments, and Games?

See [Core Gameplay](/doc/playza-gaming/core-gameplay) for a full breakdown of the four formats.

### How is the winner decided?

By the scoring system for the specific game — published on the [Rules & Scoring](/doc/help/rules-and-scoring) page.

### Why didn't I win even though I scored higher?

Check that you were in the **same contest** as the other player — different contests, even on the same game, are ranked separately.
