
# Rules & Scoring

This page lists the rules that apply across **every** Playza contest, plus pointers to the scoring details for individual games.

## Universal rules

1. **One account per person.** Multi-accounting is not allowed.
2. **No bots, scripts, or automation.** Every match must be played by a real person on a real device.
3. **No collusion.** Coordinating with other players (e.g., in H2H or Tournaments) to manipulate outcomes is prohibited.
4. **Fair connectivity.** Intentionally disconnecting to avoid a loss may count as a forfeit.
5. **No exploits.** If you find a bug, report it to [Admin@playza.games](mailto:Admin@playza.games) — don't farm it. We reward responsible disclosure.
6. **Local law applies.** You're responsible for ensuring Playza is permitted in your jurisdiction.

## Contest rules

| Rule | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Same challenge, every player** | Within a single contest, every entrant plays the same configuration / seed. |
| **Fixed prize structure** | The prize curve is shown before you enter and doesn't change mid-contest. |
| **Prize pool scales fairly** | The Playza prize pool algorithm scales from 5 to 50,000 players — bottom-tier winners still earn meaningfully. |
| **No mid-contest entry advantage** | All entrants get the same time / move budget / rules. |
| **Settled when the contest ends** | Winnings hit your wallet automatically once the contest closes. |

## Format-specific rules

### H2H — Head to Head

- Two players, one contest.
- Both pay the same $ZA entry.
- Higher score takes the prize pool minus the platform fee.
- See [Core Gameplay → H2H](/doc/playza-gaming/core-gameplay#-h2h--head-to-head).

### SoloEarn

- Solo challenge with a **set target score**.
- Beat the target, earn $ZA.
- No opponent — you're playing against the target.
- See [Core Gameplay → SoloEarn](/doc/playza-gaming/core-gameplay#-soloearn).

### Tournaments

- Multi-player contest with a shared prize pool.
- Payouts follow a **dynamic curve** — the bigger the tournament, the more winners.
- See [Core Gameplay → Tournaments](/doc/playza-gaming/core-gameplay#-tournaments).

### Games (Leaderboards)

- Players compete over a set time period.
- Ranking is based on **cumulative score**.
- Top players at end of period win $ZA rewards.
- See [Core Gameplay → Games](/doc/playza-gaming/core-gameplay#-games-leaderboards).

## Loyalty (PZA Points) rules

- PZA Points are earned through activity — see [How to Earn PZA Points](/doc/loyalty/earning-points).
- Points earned through **fraud, bot use, or multi-accounting** will be revoked.
- Referrals must be **verified real users** — self-referral or fake account referrals will be forfeited.
- See [PZA Points & Loyalty](/doc/loyalty/overview) for full program details.

## Disputes & reviews

- If you think a contest outcome is wrong, open a dispute from the contest result screen within **{/* TODO: window, e.g., 48 hours */}**.
- For urgent issues, email [Admin@playza.games](mailto:Admin@playza.games) with the contest ID.
- Disputes are reviewed by the Playza team. We may look at game logs, scores, and device data to make a call.
- Decisions on disputes are final.

## Penalties for breaking the rules

| Severity | Possible action |
| --- | --- |
| Minor | Warning, removal from the affected contest |
| Moderate | Temporary suspension, $ZA forfeiture for the affected contest, PZA Points revocation |
| Severe | Permanent ban, forfeiture of unwithdrawn balance and all PZA Points |

> We'd much rather have you in the game than out of it. Play fair, win clean.
