
# Responsible Gaming

Playza is meant to be fun. If it stops being fun, we want to help.

## Our commitments

- We design Playza to reward **skill**, not compulsive spending.
- We don't run dark patterns, fake countdowns, or pressure tactics.
- Our prize-pool algorithm scales fairly so bottom-tier winners still earn meaningfully — you don't need to keep chasing.
- We point players to professional help when they ask for it.

## Built-in guardrails

Even without changing any settings, Playza has structural guardrails:

- **Max 3 withdrawals per day** (see [Withdrawals](/doc/za-currency/withdrawals)).
- **Admin approval required for withdrawals ≥ 10,000 $ZA** — an intentional cool-down on big movements.
- **Transparent contest fees** — shown before every entry so there are no surprise stakes.

## Warning signs to watch for

- You're playing longer than you planned to, repeatedly.
- You're depositing more than you can comfortably afford to lose.
- You're hiding your play from friends or family.
- You feel anxious, irritated, or low when you can't play.
- You're chasing losses with bigger deposits.

If any of these resonate, take a break. The platform will still be here when you come back.

## A note for friends and family

If someone you care about may be struggling, you can:

- Encourage them to use the in-app limits / cooling-off tools.
