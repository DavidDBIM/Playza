
# FAQs

## About Playza

### What is Playza?

Playza is a **skill-based competitive gaming platform** where players compete across H2H, SoloEarn, Tournaments, and Games (leaderboards) using **$ZA**, our in-platform currency, and earn **PZA Points** for loyalty. See [Welcome](/) and [Key Summary](/doc/introduction/key-summary).

### Is Playza a casino?

No. Playza is **skill-based**, not chance-based. The better player wins more often than not, and every contest's rules and scoring are public.

### Is Playza a cryptocurrency project?

Not today. Playza is a fiat-funded platform and $ZA is currently an in-platform currency, not a cryptocurrency. That said, **$ZA token, NFTs, and exchange listing are targeted for Q4 2026** — see [The Future of $ZA](/doc/za-currency/future) and the [Roadmap](/doc/playza-gaming/roadmap).

### Where can I play?

At [playza.games](https://www.playza.games/) from any modern browser. A mobile app is coming soon. Global expansion is a **Q2 2026** milestone.

## Money & $ZA

### Is $ZA real money?

$ZA is a real in-platform currency that you can deposit into and withdraw from via Paystack, Opay, and bank transfer. See [What is $ZA?](/doc/za-currency/what-is-za).

### How much is 1 $ZA worth?

**1,000 $ZA = ₦1,000 = ~$0.73 USD.** Same rate for deposits and withdrawals.

### What payment methods can I use?

**Paystack, Opay, and bank transfer.** International payment methods are on the roadmap for Q2 2026.

### What are the deposit and withdrawal limits?

| | Deposit | Withdrawal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Minimum | 1,000 $ZA | 1,000 $ZA |
| Maximum | 10,000,000 $ZA/day | 3 withdrawals/day |
| Fee | 1% | None |

### How fast are withdrawals?

- **1,000 – 9,999 $ZA:** instant payout.
- **10,000 $ZA and above:** requires admin approval for security. You'll be notified once processed.

### Are there hidden fees?

No. Playza charges a **1% deposit fee** and a **transparent platform fee on paid contests**, shown before you enter. Withdrawals are free.

## Contests & games

### What are the four contest formats?

- **H2H** — two players, higher score wins.
- **SoloEarn** — beat a set target score to earn $ZA.
- **Tournaments** — multi-player shared prize pool, dynamic payout curve.
- **Games** — leaderboards over a set time period.

See [Core Gameplay](/doc/playza-gaming/core-gameplay).

### How do I know a contest is fair?

- Every player in a contest plays the same challenge.
- Scoring is public and pre-published.
- Scores are recorded server-side, not trusted from the client.
- Prize pools scale fairly from 5 to 50,000 players.

### Which games are live right now?

7 games across Arcade, Puzzle, and Strategy: **3D Stack Ball, Emoji Pop Arena, Bubble Shooter Blitz, 2048 Master, Chess, Memory Playza, Playza Snake**. See [Our Games](/doc/playza-gaming/our-games).

### Can I play for free?

You can browse and explore for free. Paid contests require $ZA entries. Separately, **PZA Points are 100% free to earn** through daily streaks, referrals, social tasks, and more — see [How to Earn PZA Points](/doc/loyalty/earning-points).

## PZA Points & Loyalty

### What are PZA Points?

Playza's loyalty score. Earned through activity, redeemable for merchandise. **PZA Points are not the same as $ZA.** See [PZA Points & Loyalty](/doc/loyalty/overview).

### What can I redeem PZA Points for?

Playza merchandise — Cap (8,000 PZA), T-Shirt (12,000 PZA), Bag (20,000 PZA). See [Rewards Hub](/doc/loyalty/rewards-hub).

### Can I convert PZA Points to $ZA?

No. They're two separate systems. PZA Points aren't withdrawable as cash.

### How do I become a Playza Ambassador?

Reach Gold tier (25,000+ PZA Points) or above, then apply through your [loyalty dashboard](https://www.playza.games/loyalty) or email [Admin@playza.games](mailto:Admin@playza.games). See [Ambassador Program](/doc/loyalty/ambassador).

## Account & security

### How do I keep my account safe?

- Don't share your login details.
- Enable strong passwords / any 2FA option offered on your account.
- Be wary of anyone claiming to be Playza support outside official channels ([Admin@playza.games](mailto:Admin@playza.games) or our official social accounts).

### I think someone else is using my account

Lock it from your device settings and contact [Admin@playza.games](mailto:Admin@playza.games) immediately.
